Data center fabric

Documentation and handover of a large Cisco ACI fabric

Challenge
A large ACI fabric had grown for years without a complete, current picture of how it was built.
Approach
Documented the fabric end to end, from physical links to tenants, bridge domains and endpoint groups, with interactive topology views and a structured handover document for the operations team.
Result
A documented operational baseline the team can plan future changes against.
Platforms
Cisco ACI, Nexus 9000
Firewall migration

Firewall hardware refresh in a production data center

Challenge
A firewall cluster running multiple virtual domains had reached the end of its hardware life.
Approach
Migrated to new hardware while keeping the virtual domains, single sign-on, centralized administrator authentication and central management in place.
Result
New hardware in service with segmentation, authentication and central management carried over.
Platforms
Fortinet FortiGate, FortiManager
VPN migration

Phased move of site-to-site VPNs to new firewall clusters

Challenge
Dozens of partner and site VPNs had to move to new firewall clusters without breaking the services behind them.
Approach
Inventoried every tunnel, grouped them into phases and moved them on a planned timeline, coordinating with the remote parties at each step.
Result
A phased plan that kept each change window to a small, testable group of tunnels.
Platforms
IPsec site-to-site VPN, multi-vendor firewalls

Troubleshooting

Fabric-wide fault storm traced to a virtualization change

Challenge
A data center fabric suddenly raised a large number of faults with no network change behind them.
Approach
Traced the cause to a change on the virtualization platform that broke its integration with the fabric, and documented the fix and how to prevent it.
Result
Root cause identified and a prevention step documented for future platform changes.
Platforms
Cisco ACI, VMware vCenter
Monitoring

Options for replacing a monitoring platform being retired

Challenge
The network team's commercial monitoring platform was being decommissioned.
Approach
Prepared replacement proposals covering metrics and logs, configuration backup, flow analysis and fabric analytics, built mainly on open-source tools.
Result
A set of options the team could compare on coverage, cost and effort.

Firewall operations

Security log collectors close to full

Approach
Found that retention settings did not match real log volumes, cleaned up and upgraded the collectors and corrected retention so it would not recur.

FAQ

Common questions

How do you staff larger projects?

Every engagement is led by our principal engineer. When the scope needs more capacity, we add vetted specialist engineers from our network. They sign the same NDAs, follow the same change and documentation standards, and report through one point of contact, so you never manage several contractors.

Can you work under our brand?

Yes. For MSPs and integrators we can work as part of your team, using your email domain, ticketing system and customer communication rules.

How fast can you start?

Scoping calls are usually available within a few business days. Overnight change work is scheduled once your change is approved.

Do you work on site?

Work is delivered remotely. On-site visits in the US can be arranged for cutovers or workshops when the project needs it.

How is pricing set?

Fixed-scope services get a fixed price after the scoping call. Ongoing support and overflow work are billed at a day rate or as a monthly block.

Which vendors do you not cover?

If your environment is built mainly on a platform we do not list, we will say so on the first call and, where we can, point you to someone who does.
