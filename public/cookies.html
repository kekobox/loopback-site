Cookie Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

This policy explains how loopbackengineering.com uses cookies and similar technologies.

Our approach

We do not use cookies for analytics, advertising, personalization or tracking, and the Site does not store anything in your browser's local storage. Because we only use what is strictly necessary to deliver and protect the Site, we do not show a cookie consent banner.

Strictly necessary cookies

Our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, may set a small number of cookies to distinguish people from automated traffic and to protect the Site from attacks. These cookies do not identify you, are not used for tracking across websites, and are required for the Site to be delivered securely.

Third-party resources

The Site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. Google receives your IP address when your browser requests the fonts. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may cause security checks to repeat or prevent parts of the Site from loading.

Changes

If we ever add analytics or other non-essential cookies, we will update this policy and ask for your consent before setting them where the law requires it.
